About XomAI

XomAI is building intelligent AI systems.

XomAI is an early-stage AI company building systems designed to learn, adapt, and improve through experience. ERIX is the first product of that work — an AI behavioral-learning layer currently in early development, focused on giving AI a structured way to learn behavioral patterns, verify them, monitor their outcomes, and determine what should actually be adopted.

The Problem

AI can learn. But should it learn everything?

A system can receive feedback, observe interactions, or discover a pattern that seems to work. That alone doesn't answer the harder questions.

01Is the pattern actually useful?
02Should it become future behavior?
03Does it work consistently?
04Was it verified?
05What happens when it stops working?
ERIX explores the missing control layer between learning and behavioral adoption.

Research

We're researching a different way for AI to adapt.

XomAI is exploring the mechanisms that would let a controlled behavioral layer work in practice. None of these problems are solved — they're the open questions we're working through.

Behavioral learning

Identifying candidate patterns worth considering, without assuming they're worth keeping.

Verification

Testing whether a candidate behavior actually satisfies defined requirements.

Outcome monitoring

Tracking accepted behaviors after adoption, not just at the moment of approval.

Controlled adaptation

Letting behavior evolve without letting it drift unchecked.

Behavioral memory

Representing accepted behavior as structured, inspectable records.

Human-defined boundaries

Keeping the definition of acceptable behavior in human hands.

Vision

What if AI could learn from experience — without blindly becoming whatever it experiences?

That's the long-term question behind ERIX. If a controlled behavioral layer works, it could eventually apply anywhere adaptive systems need to change carefully rather than freely.

Privacy

How information is handled, honestly.

This page covers what's actually true today. As ERIX moves from prototype to product, this section will be updated before any new data practice goes live.

This website, today

This prototype does not use analytics or tracking cookies. The Early Access form on this page is not currently connected to a backend — text entered into it is not transmitted, stored, or sent to anyone. It exists to preview the eventual sign-up experience.

The future ERIX product

Once ERIX moves into selected-user testing, it will need a real data policy — including how interactions are collected, what's used as candidate behavioral input, and what a user can see or remove. Those practices haven't been built yet, and we won't claim otherwise. This page will be updated before that happens, not after.
